Support

Tema keeps drafts, notes, stickers, finished films, and render balance on this device. There is no account to recover and no live support inbox. Questions can be sent through the App Store product page for Tema.

A film did not export

If you cancel rendering, or if rendering fails, the draft stays in Tema and a render is not used. Check that the draft has a name and at least one clip, then preview again. Preview itself does not use a render.

Purchases

Pack prices shown in Tema are fixed. A cancelled, failed, or unverified purchase does not add renders. These packs are consumable. Tema does not include a restore button. Purchase history for a charge can be reviewed in your Apple account.

Camera, microphone, photos, and speech

Tema asks for each of these only when you tap the matching action. If you previously chose not to allow one of them, open the system Settings app, find Tema, and turn that access on. The You page does not list permission status.

Sign out

Sign Out returns you to the opening screen and leaves your films on this device. Clear All Data is separate. It removes Tema's drafts, films, media, render balance, and purchase records after you confirm.
