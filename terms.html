The app

Tema lets you start a daily log, record a clip or import one photo or video, order clips, write or dictate notes, add stickers, choose a cover and a preview filter, preview the film, and confirm an export. You can also compile finished logs from the past week or the past month into one film. Tema does not provide an account, a public profile, a follower graph, or a cloud backup of your films.

The opening screen is the way back into the app after you sign out. Signing out does not delete your logs. Clear All Data is a separate action and is described below.

Tema stores a copy of imported and recorded media in its private storage so the draft can be edited without changing the original in your photo library. Deleting a film inside Tema removes Tema's copy. It does not delete the original item in your photo library. Saving a finished film adds a new copy to your photo library only after you tap Save Video and allow that access.

Preview and renders

Preview is free and does not use a render. Confirming an export uses one render, and only after the film file has been saved on this device. If you cancel the export, or if the export fails, the draft remains and no render is used. A week film or a month film also uses one render when you confirm it, and it can be created only when at least one finished log falls in that range and your balance has at least one render.

The first install starts with a balance of zero. Tema does not add complimentary renders.

Sign out and Clear All Data

Change the period before it expires if you want to keep the film longer inside Tema. You can also save a copy to your photo library before the period ends.
