Privacy Policy

This policy describes how the Tema app handles information on the device where you install it. Tema is a private daily film log. You record or import short clips, arrange them, add notes and stickers, preview the result, and export a film that stays on that device.

Tema does not create an account, does not run a social feed, and does not upload your films, notes, or photos to a Tema server.

Information stored on this device

The following stay in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Draft and finished film titles, creation time, status, filter name, cover choice, and clip order.
  • Clip files you record or import, including still photos turned into short clips and videos you record in the app.
  • Notes you type or dictate for a clip.
  • Stickers you add, including the sticker type and the corner where you place it.
  • The clip length you choose in Studio, and how long finished films are kept.
  • A local render balance and purchase records that identify the pack name, the fixed price shown in the app, the number of renders, and the Apple transaction identifier used so the same transaction is not counted twice.

Signing out returns you to the opening screen. It does not delete films, drafts, notes, or the render balance. Clear All Data removes drafts, finished films, media files saved by Tema, the render balance, and purchase records from this device. Deleting the app also removes that private storage. Neither action changes the original photos or videos that remain in your photo library.

Camera

Tema asks for camera access only after you tap Record. The camera is used to capture a short clip for the draft you are editing. Tema does not turn the camera on at launch, and it does not offer a separate camera test on the You page.

Microphone

Tema asks for microphone access only after you start a recording, or after you tap Speak Note. A recording can continue without audio if you decline the microphone. Speak Note needs the microphone to hear the note you dictate.

Photo library

Tema asks to read your photo library only after you tap Import, so you can choose one photo or video for the current draft. Tema asks to add to your photo library only after you tap Save Video on a finished film. Importing copies the selected item into Tema's private storage. Saving a film adds a copy to your library. Tema does not scan the rest of your library.

Speech recognition

Tema asks for speech recognition only after you tap Speak Note. The recognizer uses English. When this device supports on-device recognition, Tema requests that the note be recognized on the device. If on-device recognition is not available, Apple's speech recognition service may process the audio of that note in order to return text. You can type a note instead. Tema does not keep a separate audio file of the dictated note after the text is written into the draft.

What Tema does not request

Tema does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not read an advertising identifier. Tema does not request notification permission, does not schedule notifications, and does not register for remote notifications. Tema does not show permission or notification status on the You page.

Purchases

Packs are consumable in-app purchases sold by Apple. Payment, receipts, and refunds are handled by Apple under Apple's terms. Tema does not receive your card number, billing address, or Apple ID password. After Apple verifies a purchase, Tema adds the matching number of renders to the balance stored on this device. Prices shown in Tema are fixed US dollar amounts. Preview does not use a render. A finished export uses one render only after the film file is saved. A cancelled or failed purchase does not add renders. Consumable renders are not a subscription and are not restored from the App Store. If a transaction is later revoked, unused renders from that transaction are removed. Finished films are not deleted because a purchase was revoked.

Week and month films

When you compile a week or a month, Tema creates a new draft on this device from clips that already belong to finished logs in that range. That compile uses one render when you confirm the export. It does not send those clips off the device.

Retention

You can keep finished films for 7, 30, 90, or 365 days. When that period passes, Tema can remove the expired finished film from its private storage during normal use. Drafts you have not finished are not removed by that setting.

Children

Tema is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the App Store product page for Tema. This policy does not publish a separate support inbox.
